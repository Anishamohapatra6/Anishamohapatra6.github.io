Anisha Mohapatra

MS student in Robotics, University of Michigan

I'm a Master's student in Robotics at the University of Michigan, Ann Arbor. My research interests are in controls, multi-agent systems, optimization, and robot learning.

During the summer of 2026, before starting at Michigan, I worked remotely with Prof. Max Simchowitz at Carnegie Mellon University on the DexterLab project, which introduced me to robotics research in learning and manipulation. Before that, I spent a year as a Research Project Associate at the AI & Robotics Lab, IISc Bangalore, under Prof. Suresh Sundaram, working on optimization-based planning for Urban Air Mobility — vertiport siting, airspace design, and multi-eVTOL scheduling — through mathematical modeling and simulation. This work led to an accepted manuscript, "Vertiport Location Optimisation for Urban Air Mobility under Time-Varying Traffic and Passenger Behavior," at the IEEE Aerospace, Space and Defence Conference 2026, and another, "Mathematical Modeling Algorithm for Multi-UAV Scheduling" (with Shridhar Velhal, LTU Sweden, and Prof. Suresh Sundaram), currently in preparation.

I completed my B.Tech in Mechanical Engineering at NIT Rourkela, where my thesis focused on nonlinear 6-DOF modeling of an autonomous underwater vehicle in MATLAB/Simulink for navigation and control under ocean-current disturbances. I also worked on ROS-based navigation, manipulation, and control for an autonomous agricultural robot through the e-Yantra Robotics Challenge. Outside of research, I train in fine arts and enjoy public speaking.

Dynamics of an Autonomous Underwater Vehicle Under Ocean Currents

Undergraduate thesis, NIT Rourkela · advised by Prof. Hara Prasad Roy
Aug 2023 – May 2024
