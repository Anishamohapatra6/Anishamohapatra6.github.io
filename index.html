Anisha Mohapatra

MS student in Robotics, University of Michigan

I'm a Master's student in Robotics at the University of Michigan, Ann Arbor, working at the intersection of optimization, controls, and aerial robotics. Before Michigan, I spent a year as a Research Project Associate at the AI & Robotics Lab, IISc Bangalore, building planning and scheduling frameworks for Urban Air Mobility, and worked in industry at ITC Limited leading factory automation projects. I did my undergraduate degree in Mechanical Engineering at NIT Rourkela, with a thesis on the dynamics of autonomous underwater vehicles under ocean currents.

My research interests sit between algorithm design and hardware: optimization for multi-agent and aerial systems, robot perception, and mechatronic design. Outside of research, I train in fine arts and enjoy public speaking.

Dynamics of an Autonomous Underwater Vehicle Under Ocean Currents

Undergraduate thesis, NIT Rourkela · advised by Prof. Hara Prasad Roy
Aug 2023 – May 2024
  • Derived a nonlinear 6-DOF AUV model — body–earth frame transforms, Euler-angle kinematics, Newton–Euler dynamics — with hydrodynamic and current terms.
  • Implemented the model in MATLAB/Simulink with added mass, drag, buoyancy, and actuator dynamics.
  • Found that a 1.2 m/s current significantly increases lateral path deviation and depth error compared to still water, under identical thruster inputs.
